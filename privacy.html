Privacy policy

dashboard is a personal tool used only by its owner, Victor (matvict@gmail.com).

With the owner's permission it reads his Google Tasks through the Google Tasks API to show the tasks due today, and marks a task completed when he asks it to. Task data is read live and is not stored, sold or shared with anyone. The only thing kept is the OAuth refresh token, on the owner's own server.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
